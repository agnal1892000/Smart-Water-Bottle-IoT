# 💧 Smart Water Bottle Using IoT

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Smart Water Bottle Using IoT** is an IoT-based hydration monitoring system designed to help individuals monitor and improve their water intake throughout the day.

The system uses sensors to monitor the **water level and temperature** of the water inside the bottle. The collected information is processed by a built-in computer and can provide real-time feedback, hydration reminders, and information about the user's progress toward their daily water intake goals.

The system is designed to make hydration tracking convenient and help users maintain healthy hydration habits.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of the Smart Water Bottle are:

* Monitor and track water consumption.
* Measure the water level inside the bottle.
* Monitor the temperature of the water.
* Provide customizable hydration reminders.
* Provide personalized hydration feedback and recommendations.
* Display hydration information through a connected application.
* Help users maintain their daily hydration goals.

---

## 🔧 Key Components

### 1. 🌡️ Temperature Sensor

The temperature sensor monitors the temperature of the water inside the bottle.

The collected temperature information can be used to provide personalized recommendations based on the user's requirements and preferences.

### 2. 📏 Ultrasonic Sensor

The ultrasonic sensor detects the presence of water and measures the water level inside the bottle.

It uses sound waves to determine the distance between the sensor and the surface of the water. This information can be used to calculate the water level and track water consumption.

### 3. 💧 Water Level Sensor

The water level sensor detects the amount of water remaining in the bottle.

When the water level becomes low, the system can alert the user and provide a reminder to refill the bottle.

### 4. 📱 Mobile Application

A connected mobile application can be used to:

* Track water intake.
* Display hydration information.
* Receive hydration reminders.
* Monitor progress toward daily water intake goals.

---

## 🔄 Working Principle

The system works through the following process:

1. The user fills the smart bottle with water.
2. The smart water bottle is switched on.
3. The water-level sensor detects the amount of water.
4. The temperature sensor measures the water temperature.
5. Sensor data is transmitted to the built-in computer.
6. The built-in computer processes the collected data.
7. The system calculates the user's hydration progress.
8. The system provides feedback and reminders to the user.
9. The information can be displayed through a connected smartphone application.
